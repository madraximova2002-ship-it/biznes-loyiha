📐 EOQ formulasi

EOQ = √(2 × D × S / H)

D = yillik talab, S = buyurtma xarajati, H = saqlash xarajati

📐 ROP formulasi

ROP = (d × L) + SS

d = kunlik talab, L = yetkazish muddati, SS = xavfsizlik zaxirasi

📐 Safety Stock

SS = Z × σ × √L

Z = 1.645 (95%), σ = standart og'ish, L = yetkazish muddati
